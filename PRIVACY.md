# Privacy

Effective: 18 November 2026
Owner: Dr Sohil Momin — sohilmomin2000@gmail.com
Changes: Initial version.

## What this app is

A single-player arena shooter that runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no server, no
sign-in and no backend of any kind.

## What it collects

Nothing. No analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no
advertising identifiers.

## What it stores on your device

| Key | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| `hellgate.best` | Best run: waves survived, kills, seed | Until you clear site data |

Nothing else is written. The value never leaves your browser and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Network requests

The page requests only its own files: HTML, the ES modules in `play/src/`, the vendored Three.js build
in `play/vendor/`, and the images in `assets/`. There are no third-party requests, no fonts, no CDNs
and no trackers.

## Children

No data is collected from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

Material changes are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` and bump the version above.
